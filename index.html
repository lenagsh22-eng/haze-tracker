    How alerts work

    The app checks NEA's readings whenever it's open (every 5 minutes) and notifies you when your region's PSI moves above your threshold, and again when it drops back.

    On Android Chrome, once the app is installed, it can also check in the background roughly once an hour if your phone allows it. On iPhone, alerts only arrive while the app is open, since iOS needs a push server for background alerts.

    Add to your home screen

    On iPhone: tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. Open the app from there to turn on notifications.

    Install Haze Tracker like an app, with its own icon and full-screen view.
